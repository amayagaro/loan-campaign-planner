Who should we call first?

A neural network scores each of 1,000 customers on how likely they are to accept a personal loan. Set how many calls your team can make and the list ranks who to phone.

This is a back-test: the 1,000 customers below are past prospects whose answers are known, so you can see how accurate the ranking was.

How to read these numbers

Score: the model’s estimate of the chance that a customer accepts.

Precision: of the customers you call, the share who accept.

Recall: of everyone who would accept, the share on your list.

Lift: acceptances found compared with calling in random order.

Priority: High is a score of 50% or more, Medium is 20% to 50%, Low is below 20%.
